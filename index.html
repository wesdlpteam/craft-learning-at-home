About these curriculum topics

Curriculum topics are parent-friendly adaptations of Australian Curriculum Version 9.0, Foundation–Year 10. They cover content descriptions, not every teaching example or achievement standard. Language frameworks do not supply a full course for every named language. Years 11–12 offer separate IB Diploma and VCE course outlines, including languages, DP core, Vocational Major and VET pathways. Senior summaries are original navigation aids based on public IB and VCAA sources, not full syllabuses. School-based and qualification-specific courses use a general starting prompt. General capabilities are not a separate topic library. Schools may teach topics in a different order.
